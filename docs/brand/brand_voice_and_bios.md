# Brand Voice

## Personality

Welcoming, outdoorsy, capable, local, community-first, and practical. The club should sound like an experienced trail friend: enthusiastic without hype, knowledgeable without talking down to people, and encouraging without being reckless.

## Writing principles

1. Lead with the trail, place, or experience.
2. Keep logistics clear: distance, difficulty, weather, meeting point, and expectations.
3. Be welcoming to different skill levels while being specific about hike difficulty.
4. Emphasize respect for trails, wildlife, private property, and Leave No Trace practices.
5. Avoid overusing slang, exclamation points, and generic motivational language.

## Recommended tagline

**Explore Tennessee. Together.**

Optional alternates:

- Find Your Trail. Find Your People.
- Tennessee Trails. Shared Adventures.
- Mountains, Trails, Community.

## Short social bio

Tennessee trails, mountains & community. Group hikes, outdoor inspiration and a place for hikers to connect across the Volunteer State.

## Very short bio

Tennessee trails • mountains • community

## Website meta description

A community for people who love exploring Tennessee trails, mountains, waterfalls, and the outdoors.
